Car Wash Manager

Offline-first bookkeeping for car, bike, and auto-rickshaw wash businesses.

Car Wash Manager is an Android application for owners, cashiers, and staff to record vehicle washes, track payments, and review business activity. Everyday bookkeeping works without an internet connection.

Manage your wash business

  • Record vehicles, wash types, prices, staff, and cash or online payments.
  • Use typed or optional voice entry to capture wash details.
  • Review wash history and business statistics.
  • Import, export, and share records when you choose.

Business records are stored locally in a SQLite database on your device. Feature availability varies by edition.

Optional Google Drive backup and restore

The paid edition can back up your business database to your own Google Drive and restore a selected backup. Google sign-in is used for this optional feature; it is not required for everyday offline bookkeeping.

When you connect a Google account, the App requests permission to manage only the specific Drive files created or opened with this app (drive.file). It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Backups are saved as SQLite database files in a folder named Car Wash Manager Backups. You can request a backup manually or enable daily automatic backup. Turning off automatic backup or signing out does not delete existing cloud backups.

Read our Privacy Policy for details about Google account information, backup retention and deletion, diagnostics, and speech recognition.
