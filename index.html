Privacy Policy for Car Wash Manager

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

Car Wash Manager ("we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-first bookkeeping application for vehicle wash businesses.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, where it goes, why it is used, and how long it remains available.

1. Business Data and Local Storage

The App stores wash records, vehicle details, prices, payment methods, staff names, and notes in a SQLite database on your device.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your business data. The App only transfers business data when you select an export, sharing, or backup feature.

The paid edition offers optional Google Sheets backup. If you use it, the App transfers selected wash records to a spreadsheet in your Google account.

Google Sign-In gives the App your account name, email address, profile photo, and an authorization token. The App uses them only for the backup feature.

2. Crash Reports, Performance Data, and Logs

The App uses Sentry to find crashes, diagnose errors, and measure performance. This diagnostic collection occurs automatically while you use the App.

Sentry may receive crash reports, stack traces, diagnostic logs, technical identifiers, device model, operating-system version, app version, and performance timings.

The App sends a 20% sample of performance transactions. It does not use Sentry Session Replay and does not send screen recordings to Sentry.

The Sentry configuration disables default personal-information collection. Diagnostic events can still contain technical data related to an error.

Sentry processes this data for us as a service provider. For more information, read the Sentry Privacy Policy.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition

The App requests microphone permission when you start voice entry. You can deny this permission and type the wash entry instead.

The App uses the device speech-recognition service and prefers Google's service on Android. The service converts your speech into text.

Depending on your device and speech-service configuration, audio can be processed on your device or sent to the provider's servers.

The App does not save an audio recording. The speech provider controls its own processing and retention under the provider's privacy policy.

4. Exporting and Sharing

The App accesses storage or the document picker when you choose to import, export, save, or share a file.

A receiving application, storage provider, or person controls a file after you share or save it outside the App.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Sentry: Processes crash reports, performance data, and diagnostic logs.
  • Google speech recognition: Can process speech audio and return text on supported Android devices.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Sheets: Support the optional backup feature in the paid edition.
  • Device platform services: Provide speech recognition, permissions, storage access, and document sharing.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not use these services to sell data or deliver advertising.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Clearing the App data or uninstalling the App erases the local SQLite database.
  • Exported files remain where you saved or shared them until you delete them.
  • Google Sheets backups remain in your Google account until you delete the spreadsheet.
  • Sentry retains diagnostic data according to our Sentry project configuration and Sentry's retention rules.
  • A speech provider can retain data according to its privacy policy and your account or device configuration.

Contact us if you have a question or deletion request about data that we control.

7. Data Security

The App uses encrypted HTTPS connections when it communicates with Sentry, Google Sign-In, and Google Sheets.

No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security. Protect your device, Google account, exports, and backups from unauthorized access.

8. Children's Privacy

Car Wash Manager is intended for business bookkeeping. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We can update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. This page will show the latest revision date.
