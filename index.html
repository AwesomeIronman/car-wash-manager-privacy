Privacy Policy for Car Wash Manager

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Car Wash Manager ("we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-first bookkeeping and wash record application designed for car and bike wash centers, managers, and service bay operators.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, device permissions, and local storage.

1. Data Handling & Storage

Business & Bookkeeping Data: All primary operational records—including vehicle entries, wash types, amounts, and customer logs—are stored locally on your device via SQLite. You maintain complete ownership and control over your records. We do not sell, rent, or trade your business data.

Technical & Diagnostic Data: To monitor app performance, troubleshoot crashes, and enhance user experience, the App may collect anonymous, aggregated technical metrics (such as device model, operating system version, app version, performance diagnostics, and general usage statistics). This data is non-personally identifiable and is used solely for operational reliability.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

The App requests only the permissions necessary to provide its core features:

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO / NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription): Used exclusively when you tap the microphone button to dictate wash entries hands-free. Voice audio is processed in real time using native speech recognition on your device. Audio recordings are not permanently stored or used for marketing purposes.
  • Storage & Document Sharing: Used solely when you explicitly request to export your records (e.g., generating and sharing an Excel spreadsheet) or import a backup file.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

We may utilize standard, trusted third-party developer services and libraries (such as crash reporting, performance monitoring, or platform utilities) to help maintain app quality and diagnose technical issues. These services process limited diagnostic data in accordance with their respective privacy standards.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored strictly on your local device:

  • Uninstalling the App or clearing the App data in your device settings will permanently erase all local records.
  • You can export and back up your records anytime using the built-in Excel export feature.

5. Children's Privacy

Car Wash Manager is intended for business bookkeeping and commercial operation. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.
